I am a final-year AI undergraduate with **four research internships** spanning diverse domains from temporal graph neural networks for financial prediction to multimodal deep learning for cancer immunotherapy. My research journey reflects a deliberate exploration toward understanding **principled machine learning methods** that bridge theoretical foundations with real-world impact.

I also develop **synthetic datasets and evaluation benchmarks** for healthcare, scientific, and language applications.

## 🧪 Synthetic Dataset Work

I develop synthetic datasets and evaluation benchmarks across healthcare, science, and language applications.

### Medical & biomedical data

- **Medical imaging:** Retinal fundus and long-bone fracture images.
- **Patient trajectories:** Oncology, treatment response, survival, and longitudinal measurements.
- **Clinical text:** Triage records, microbiology reports, antimicrobial resistance, and adverse-event narratives.
- **Physiological data:** Simulated neural signals and gait telemetry.
- **Molecular data:** Enzyme sequences, reaction selectivity, and biological composition graphs.
- **Medical documents:** Fictional credentialing records and evidence reconciliation.

### Other synthetic data

- **Language & NLP:** Artificial languages, fictional narratives, financial text, and question answering.
- **Scientific data:** Molecular graphs, chemistry, physical simulations, and mathematical reasoning.
- **AI evaluation:** Document consistency, retrieval, citation grounding, hallucination, and prompt-injection detection.
- **Code & decision tasks:** Code transformations, operations, recommendations, and scenario–response pairs.

**Formats:** Images, text, time series, tables, graphs, and linked documents.

**Sample datasets can be provided upon request.**
